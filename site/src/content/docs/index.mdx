Every piece of advice in this book is a numbered recommendation, such as PCB-DFA-006.
Each one says what to do, why, and what goes wrong if you don't.
These are recommendations, not rules, and the book tries to say what you give up when you choose something else.

The book follows the path a board takes through a factory.
