## About the author

Chris Denney has worked in electronics manufacturing since he was 16.
He is the CTO of Worthington Assembly in South Deerfield, Massachusetts, and VP of Production at CircuitHub.
In 2020 he started the Pick, Place, Podcast, where he talks with people across the industry about how boards are designed and built.

He wrote this book because most of the problems he sees on the factory floor could have been avoided at the design stage, often at no cost.
He tells the story of how the book came about, in his own words, in the [Preface](/preface/).

## How the book was written

The author used AI tools to help draft the text and drawings.
He reviewed every recommendation himself before publishing.

## Corrections and contributions

OpenDFX is open source, and it gets better with every correction.
If you find a mistake, or you build boards differently and can say why, please [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/OpenDFX/OpenDFX/issues/new/choose).
Pull requests are welcome too.
The [contribution guide](https://github.com/OpenDFX/OpenDFX/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) explains how changes are reviewed, and everyone taking part follows the [code of conduct](https://github.com/OpenDFX/OpenDFX/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
Contributors are credited in the [change log](/changelog/).
For anything else, write to [hello@opendfx.org](mailto:hello@opendfx.org).
