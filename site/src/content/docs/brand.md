Writing about OpenDFX, linking to it or building on it?
Here are the logo, the colors and the name, ready to use.

**[Download the press kit](/opendfx-brand.zip)** (ZIP), with every logo below as SVG and PNG, the colors, and a short guide.
Every file is also listed on this page.

## The logo

<img class="dark:sl-hidden" src="/brand/opendfx-logo-green.svg" alt="The OpenDFX logo: a green square tile holding the symbol, beside the name OpenDFX in bold letters." width="480" />
<img class="light:sl-hidden" src="/brand/opendfx-logo-green-on-dark.svg" alt="The OpenDFX logo: a green square tile holding the symbol, beside the name OpenDFX in bold letters." width="480" />

The symbol is an open solder jumper.
Two copper pads face each other across a gap, inside a white legend outline.
The outline is open where the jumper is open.
Together the pads make an O, and each pad on its own is a D.

The logo comes on two boards: green, which is the main one, and charcoal.
Both are equally official, so use whichever suits the page.

## Using the logo

Please leave clear space around the logo.
The space should be at least a quarter of the symbol's height on every side.

Don't make the symbol smaller than 16 pixels on a screen.
In print, don't make it smaller than 6 mm.

Please don't change the logo:

- Don't stretch, squash or rotate it.
- Don't change its colors, except to the one-color versions above.
- Don't add outlines, shadows, glows or other effects.
- Don't set the name in another typeface, or rearrange the symbol and the name.
- Don't put the green or charcoal logo on a busy photograph. Use a one-color version instead.

## Colors

The colors come from the book's board color key, which every drawing in the book uses.

| Color | Hex | RGB | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solder-mask green | `#1E7A3E` | 30, 122, 62 | The green board, and the main brand color. |
| Charcoal | `#262A2F` | 38, 42, 47 | The dark board. |
| Copper | `#D9B44A` | 217, 180, 74 | The two pads of the jumper. |
| Silkscreen white | `#F2F2EF` | 242, 242, 239 | The legend outline around the pads. |
| Ink | `#1D2125` | 29, 33, 37 | The name on light backgrounds. |
| Light ink | `#E8EAED` | 232, 234, 237 | The name on dark backgrounds. |

The same colors are in the press kit as `colors.json`.

## The typeface

The name and the whole book are set in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, a free typeface designed for readers with low vision.
The name in the logo is its Bold weight.
The logo files hold the letters as shapes, so you don't need the font to use them.

## Writing the name

Write the name as one word, OpenDFX, with a capital O, D, F and X.
Please don't write it as "Open DFX", "OpenDfx" or "openDFX".
The book's full title is *OpenDFX for Printed Circuit Boards*.

OpenDFX is a trademark of Chris Denney.
Please write OpenDFX™ the first time the name appears in an article or post.
After that, plain OpenDFX is fine.

You're welcome to use the logo and the name to link to OpenDFX, to write about it, or to say that your work is based on it.
Please don't use them in a way that suggests Chris Denney endorses your product or service, or as part of your own logo.
The [About page](/about/#trademark) says more about how to use the name.
If you're not sure, ask at [hello@opendfx.org](mailto:hello@opendfx.org).
